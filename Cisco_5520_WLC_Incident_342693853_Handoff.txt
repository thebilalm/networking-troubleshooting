CISCO TAC CASE DETAILS / RECOMMENDED DESCRIPTION
Suggested severity: S2 / High Impact due to multi-site production wireless impact, progressive AP loss, and loss of management access while the controller remains pingable.

Suggested title:
Cisco 5520 WLC losing APs and no longer accessible via SSH/GUI

Suggested TAC description:
Cisco 5520 WLC USADC5520WLC02 (172.28.182.214), running AireOS 8.10.183.0, is experiencing an ongoing issue impacting wireless service at multiple sites. The WLC was initially reachable via SSH but not via GUI. During troubleshooting, the number of joined APs continued to decrease (initially approximately 87 APs, later observed at 64 and then 57), and multiple sites reported wireless connectivity issues.

Network connectivity to the WLC has been validated from the directly connected switch SWTADC_02. WLC uplink Port-channel26 is up/up with both Gi7/15 and Gi9/15 bundled and forwarding with no interface errors. VLAN 220 (AP Management) is active and forwarding. WLC management IP 172.28.182.214 responds successfully to ping, ARP resolves, and the WLC MAC is learned through Po26.

Routes to 10.111.1.0/24 and 10.112.1.0/24 are present and both 10.111.1.53 and 10.112.1.53 are reachable when sourced from the VLAN 220 SVI. Earlier, the WLC itself was unable to ping these destinations.

The condition has now worsened: the WLC remains pingable at 172.28.182.214, but SSH access is no longer responding and GUI access remains unavailable.

Requesting Cisco TAC assistance to investigate the WLC/controller condition and continued AP loss. Please advise on recovery steps and any additional diagnostics required.
